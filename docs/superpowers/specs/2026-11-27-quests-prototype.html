How tier shows up: the crimson frame always says "quest"; the tier rides on top of it, the same way achievements escalate. The header gem and text take the tier color, the glow grows with tier, gold+ lands with a pop and flash, platinum+ adds a light sweep, legendary+ adds a pulsing glow behind the banner, and celestial gets the star field and aurora name. In C the tier color is also one of the stops in the rotating border, and the rotation speeds up with tier. The pill gets a tier gem and a stronger glow.
